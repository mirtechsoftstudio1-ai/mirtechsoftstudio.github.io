1. About this notice

This notice describes the TimeNest Android app as currently documented in the project and the Google Mobile Ads SDK included in the project. TimeNest is intended for people aged 13 and older, including students and adults. Children under 13 are not an intended audience. The app does not verify age or prevent a younger person from using it.

The publisher intends to offer the app in Pakistan and other countries where Google Play distribution and applicable requirements permit. The final enabled country list and country-specific legal requirements have not been confirmed. This notice must be checked against the exact signed release, SDK versions, merged manifest, audience settings, countries and final advertising configuration before publication.

2. Information stored by TimeNest on this device

When you create a Letter, Goal or Memory, TimeNest stores the information you enter—such as titles, text, creation and unlock dates, Goal target dates and progress, Memory event dates, archive state and attachment references—in a local SQLite database in the app's private storage.

A photo you select for a Memory using Android's system photo picker is copied into TimeNest's app-private attachment storage. The inspected implementation has no TimeNest account service, developer-operated capsule server, cloud synchronization or in-app backup service. Capsule text and photos are not encrypted at rest by TimeNest.

The inspected implementation does not send capsule text, titles, photos, reminder preferences or capsule identifiers to a TimeNest-operated server. This does not mean third-party SDKs collect no device or advertising data; see below.

3. Reminders and notifications

Reminder preferences are stored locally. TimeNest schedules optional daily reminders and capsule unlock notifications through Android's local notification system. Unlock notifications use generic display text and include a local capsule identifier in their internal payload so the app can open the relevant record when selected. Daily reminders use generic wording.

Whether Android shows notification text on a lock screen depends on device settings. TimeNest does not send capsule text to its own server for notification delivery.

4. Google Mobile Ads and other services

The app includes Google's Google Mobile Ads Flutter plugin. The project has banner ad placements on Home, Archive and Settings and currently uses Google's official sample/test identifiers. The project lockfile records plugin version 9.2.0; a prior dependency audit recorded Android Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0. Both versions and the merged manifest must be verified against the exact release build.

Google's Android SDK disclosure for version 25.5.0 says the SDK automatically collects and shares the device's IP address (which may be used to estimate general location), product interactions such as app launches and taps, diagnostic information such as app launch time, hang rate and energy usage, and device/account identifiers such as Android Advertising ID and app set ID, and where applicable other signed-in-account identifiers. Google lists advertising, analytics and fraud prevention as purposes and says the listed SDK data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Android Advertising ID collection is optional according to Google's disclosure and may depend on device controls and manifest configuration. Google's processing and retention are governed by Google's applicable policies and settings, not by a TimeNest retention period. The final SDK version, merged manifest, ad configuration and exact Google Play Data Safety mapping must be checked before release.

The inspected app has no UMP/CMP consent flow and no in-app privacy-options entry point. This policy does not claim that a consent decision has been collected, that personalized ads are enabled or disabled, or that any particular regional consent requirement has been satisfied. Applicable choices and legal bases remain under review. Test identifiers do not establish that production ads are approved or serving.

5. How long information remains and how to delete it

TimeNest's local capsule records, Memory photos and reminder preferences remain in app storage until you delete them using available app controls, clear TimeNest's storage in Android settings or remove the app. Deleting a capsule through TimeNest also removes its managed photo attachments; the Memory interface also supports removing an individual managed attachment.

Once local data is removed, TimeNest has no server copy from which to recover it. Android/device behavior may affect data removal when app storage is cleared or the app is removed. Android backup and device-transfer of app data are disabled by the current Android configuration.

The current app does not provide a separate account-based data export, remote deletion request or in-app request form for data held by Google Mobile Ads. TimeNest does not set a retention period for Google's SDK data. Google and Android provide their own applicable controls and request processes. Your legal rights and available remedies depend on your location and the responsible publisher/controller; they have not been determined in this draft.

6. Your choices and rights

You can manage local capsule content and photos using the app's delete and attachment-removal controls, and manage Android notification permissions/settings through Android. Android may provide controls for resetting or deleting the Advertising ID where available.

The current TimeNest app has no age gate, consent form or privacy-options screen. Any additional rights—such as access, correction, restriction, objection, portability or deletion requests relating to third-party processing—depend on applicable law and the responsible publisher/controller. Do not rely on this draft as confirmation that a specific request route is currently available.
