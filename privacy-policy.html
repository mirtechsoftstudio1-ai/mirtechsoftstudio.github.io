1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TimeNest handles information when you use the TimeNest Android application.

TimeNest is designed for people aged 13 and older, including students and adults. Children under 13 are not an intended audience. The app does not verify a user's age or technically prevent someone under 13 from using it.

The publisher intends to make TimeNest available in countries where Google Play distribution and applicable requirements permit. Availability may vary by country.

This policy covers information stored locally by TimeNest and information that may be collected or processed by Google's advertising and consent-management services.

2. Information stored locally on your device

When you create a Letter, Goal or Memory, TimeNest stores the information you enter. This may include:

  • Titles and text you write.
  • Creation dates and unlock dates.
  • Goal target dates and progress.
  • Memory event dates.
  • Archive status and attachment references.
  • Reminder preferences and related settings.

This information is stored in a local SQLite database within the app's private storage.

When you select a photo for a Memory using Android's system photo picker, TimeNest copies that photo into its app-private attachment storage.

The documented app implementation does not provide a TimeNest account service, developer-operated capsule server, cloud synchronisation service or in-app cloud backup service.

Capsule text and photos are stored locally and are not encrypted at rest by TimeNest.

The use of third-party SDKs may involve separate collection and processing of device or advertising information, as explained below.

3. Reminders and notifications

TimeNest stores reminder preferences locally on your device.

The app can schedule optional daily reminders and capsule-unlock notifications through Android's local notification system.

Unlock notifications use generic notification text. Their internal payload may contain a local capsule identifier so that the app can open the relevant record when you select the notification.

TimeNest does not send capsule text to its own server for notification delivery.

Whether notification content appears on your lock screen depends on your Android device's notification and privacy settings.

You can manage notification permissions through Android settings.

4. Google Mobile Ads and consent management

TimeNest includes Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK through the Google Mobile Ads Flutter plugin. The app has banner ad placements on the Home, Archive and Settings screens.

The production release configuration uses the publisher's registered AdMob application and banner ad identifiers. Test builds may use Google's official sample/test identifiers instead.

Google's Android Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App interactions, including app launches and interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang information and device performance data.
  • Device and advertising identifiers, such as Android Advertising ID and app set ID, where applicable.

Google describes uses of this information that include advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Certain information may be shared with Google or other relevant service providers as part of the advertising service.

The exact information collected can depend on the SDK version, Android device settings, available identifiers, advertising configuration and applicable consent choices.

Consent management

TimeNest includes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent-management integration.

The app can refresh consent information, display a consent form when Google determines one is required, and check whether advertising requests may be made under the current consent state.

The app's advertising initialisation and request flow is subject to the consent information returned by the UMP SDK.

Where UMP indicates that a privacy-options entry point is required, the app can provide access to those options through its privacy-options control.

The availability and content of consent choices may vary by country and applicable requirements. This policy does not claim that every regional consent requirement has been satisfied or that personalised advertising is always enabled or disabled.

Google's processing and retention of advertising information are governed by Google's applicable policies and settings. TimeNest does not set Google's retention periods.

For more information, see:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure
  • Google UMP for Flutter
  • Google Privacy & Messaging and certified CMP information

5. Data retention and deletion

TimeNest's local capsule records, Memory photos and reminder preferences remain in the app's storage until they are deleted through available app controls, the app's storage is cleared through Android settings, or the app is uninstalled.

Deleting a capsule through TimeNest also removes its managed photo attachments. The Memory interface also supports removing an individual managed attachment.

TimeNest does not maintain a server copy of local capsule content from which deleted records can be recovered.

Android device behaviour may affect data removal when app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. The current Android configuration disables app-data backup and device-transfer through the configured backup rules.

TimeNest does not control Google's retention of information collected through Google Mobile Ads. Google provides its own applicable privacy controls and request processes.

The current app does not provide an account-based data export service or a separate in-app request form for information held by Google Mobile Ads.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

You can:

  • Create, manage and delete capsule content using the available app controls.
  • Remove managed Memory photo attachments using the available controls.
  • Change notification permissions through Android settings.
  • Use Android's available advertising-ID controls, where supported.
  • Use available consent and privacy options presented through Google's consent-management process.

Additional rights, such as access, correction, restriction, objection, portability or deletion requests relating to third-party processing, depend on the laws applicable to your location and the relevant service provider.

Some jurisdictions and platform policies may apply additional requirements to users aged 13–17. Requirements can vary depending on the user's country, the app's distribution settings and the advertising services used.

8. Security

TimeNest stores capsule content and managed photo attachments in app-private storage on the device.

The app does not encrypt capsule text or photos at rest using a separate TimeNest encryption feature.

Information transmitted by Google's advertising services is subject to the security measures applied by Google. Google's SDK disclosure describes data transmitted by the SDK as encrypted in transit using secure connections.

You should protect access to your device and use the security controls provided by your Android device.
